# Domain semantics v1

## Money and roles

The case and every approved monetary fact explicitly name `USD`. Unsupported or mixed currency is rejected before computation; no currency conversion is inferred.

Only `USD` is supported. The cross-language monetary value is an integer number of cents in the signed 64-bit range `[-9223372036854775808, 9223372036854775807]`. JSON carries it as a canonical base-10 string: `0` or `-?[1-9][0-9]*`; `-0`, leading plus, leading zeros, exponents, fractions, and JSON numeric money are invalid. Intermediate arithmetic is checked before narrowing. A positive fact amount means magnitude, not an accounting sign. `school_credit` adds to school surplus; `school_charge` subtracts. `refund_issued` and `bank_credit_observed` are distinct evidence and never silently change the school surplus metric. `aid_offer`, `aid_pending`, `balance_snapshot`, and `work_study_offer` are not posted movements.

Source decimal parsing accepts explicit US-style decimal point and optional grouped comma thousands only when grouping is valid, with at most two fractional digits. Parentheses or one leading minus may indicate a negative source value, but an adapter must state its column/sign convention before assigning a fact role. A value with both debit and credit, unknown locale, or more than two fractional digits needs review or an unsupported result. No binary float path is permitted.

The source-based school surplus for one term and one approved knowledge snapshot is `sum(current approved school_credit magnitudes) - sum(current approved school_charge magnitudes)`. It is an accounting view of uploaded/reviewed evidence, not an entitlement or an expected refund. A `refund_issued` record and an observed bank credit are reported separately. No half-year allocation or institution-specific interpretation is inferred.

## Identity and history

Artifact identity, source location, proposal identity, fact identity, event identity, real-world movement identity, and sync identity are distinct. Exact unchanged source reimport is idempotent at the reviewed-command boundary; equal amount/date/description from separate source positions is not automatically a duplicate. Original source strings are retained locally and never normalized in place. A manually entered fact remains user asserted.

An approved fact may link to one extraction proposal at the exact artifact and source position. That proposal retains its raw original value, parser/mapping versions, and nullable normalized proposed cents; the reviewed approved cents remain a separate value. One proposal cannot approve two monetary facts. An unlinked artifact fact is a manual transcription from the cited source, while a manual-source fact remains user asserted. An unreviewed proposal has no monetary effect.

Each term belongs to one institution and names one school-account reference. A posted school movement or issued refund must name that account. A bank observation names a bank-account reference and need not claim an academic term before a reviewed match decision. An artifact with a declared account cannot be used as the source of a fact assigned to a different account. The source-based school surplus query is scoped to the term's school account; bank accounts and another institution's school account cannot contribute.

The approved journal is an immutable directed acyclic graph. Each event has a unique ASCII ID, parent event IDs, an explicit UTC approval/record time, and one payload. A query identifies one or more approved heads. Its knowledge snapshot is the transitive ancestor closure of those heads, independent of input enumeration order. A wall-clock cutoff is only a mapping to recorded local snapshots; divergent offline branches can yield multiple candidates and must be labeled ambiguous rather than ordered by untrusted device clocks.

The reference `heads_as_known` mapping accepts one explicit UTC cutoff and returns `EMPTY`, `UNIQUE`, or `AMBIGUOUS` with the eligible maximal causal heads. A child timestamp earlier than a parent does not make the child available before its parent; a cutoff inside that inversion is ambiguous. This mapping describes timestamps within the supplied local case, not a globally authoritative order across unsynchronized devices. Explicit head queries remain the reproducible historical identity.

`approve_fact` introduces one reviewed fact. `correct_fact` names the fact and replaces its magnitude or cancels it; it never adds a second financial movement. Two corrections to the same fact are ordered only when one causally descends the other. Concurrent incomparable corrections produce `CONTRADICTORY_EVIDENCE` until an explicit resolution event names the chosen branch. A cycle, missing parent, duplicate event ID, or event referring to a nonexistent fact is invalid. Observation/effective dates and approval times are distinct; an unreviewed proposal has no effect at any head.

Every correction event directly names the original fact-approval event as a parent. A later correction also names the earlier correction as a parent when it supersedes that decision. This keeps causal validation bounded and makes concurrent branches visible.

The local Python reference supports single-term school surplus, per-fact attribution, reviewed bank coverage, conservative matching, and evidence-only aid-item lifecycle views. C++/WASM parity and the full historical release corpus remain open. A future engine version may reanalyze old facts, but historical reproduction uses the recorded engine/rule version.

An aid item links only facts explicitly assigned to it. Its institution, optional term, and recipient kind are local reviewed metadata, not institution authentication. Annual offers are not split into term amounts. `aid_offer`, `aid_accepted`, `aid_pending`, and `work_study_offer` remain snapshot/evidence stages, not posted credits; linked `school_credit` facts are the only posted amount in the item lifecycle. Canceled snapshots remain in observations but no longer supply an active current snapshot. Two unreconciled active offer snapshots are ambiguous. Later observed stages do not create missing earlier stages. Parent/third-party recipient metadata requires a recipient verification action before any student-account claim. The lifecycle projection never computes legal entitlement or an expected refund.

For a term-linked aid item, `aid_gross_disbursement` and `aid_fee_withheld` are distinct facts drawn from a reviewed `aid_disbursement_statement` artifact. They do not bind to a school or bank account and do not enter the school-surplus sum. The gross amount is actual disbursement evidence; an `aid_offer` is only an offer snapshot and cannot stand in for it. The lifecycle reports gross, observed withheld fee, net posted school credit, and `gross − observed fee − posted` when gross and posted evidence are present. A missing fee record remains missing, even when the arithmetic difference is zero; a nonzero difference is an unresolved finding and not an inferred fee. Multiple distinct gross or fee facts sum by source identity, and corrections retain their own evidence. These source artifacts are not externally authenticated.

## Attribution and uncertainty

For school surplus, each current fact has signed contribution `+credit` or `-charge`. Comparing two heads computes each fact's before/after contribution and groups the exact difference by fact ID. The sum must equal the overall delta exactly. A match or coverage change with no monetary movement has zero monetary contribution and may alter evidence quality separately. Conflicting corrections make the comparison incomplete, never a guessed residual.

`OBSERVED` means a source observation exists, without external authentication. `SUPPORTED_BY_UPLOADED_RECORDS` means the stated computation follows the selected reviewed uploads, without a completeness claim. `USER_ASSERTED` means a material fact depends on manual entry or user assertion. `PENDING`, `AMBIGUOUS`, `INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE`, `CONTRADICTORY_EVIDENCE`, `UNSUPPORTED_INPUT`, and `COMPUTATION_LIMIT` have distinct typed meanings. A bank CSV's transaction extrema never establish full statement-period coverage. “No matching deposit in uploaded records” cannot become “never paid.”

Bank coverage is an explicit reviewed assertion for one bank account, one `bank_transactions` record type, and a half-open date interval. A source-asserted period points to a bank-statement artifact bound to that account and a source location; a user-asserted period uses a distinct manual entry. The assertion reports what the reviewer found in the upload, without authenticating the statement. A reviewed retraction event names the original assertion and removes it from later snapshots while preserving earlier results. Coverage evaluation unions only active assertions for the queried account, reports exact missing intervals, and labels a complete period `USER_ASSERTED` if any necessary part depends on manual assertion. An observed transaction alone never establishes a covered interval.

The first candidate search window runs from the refund-issued source date through 30 calendar days later, inclusive. It is configurable within 0–90 days and is solely a search heuristic; no legal or institutional deadline is inferred. Missing dates or incomplete account-specific coverage prevent a definite statement about the absence of a deposit. Amount/date coincidence yields a suggestion only. A reviewed `decide_match` event may confirm a selected exact-cent allocation or reject a link; a later causally ordered decision can reverse it. Concurrent opposing decisions remain contradictory. Confirmed allocations cannot exceed the current refund magnitude or bank-credit magnitude across all reviewed links. A source-based period assertion does not prove that every transaction in the upload was extracted and approved, so an empty search reports `NO_CANDIDATE_IN_APPROVED_FACTS` with an explicit source-set limitation, never nonpayment. A reviewed allocation does not authenticate the source documents.

An issued refund records a reviewed intended `recipientKind`; a bank-account reference records a separately reviewed `holderKind`. These are broad recipient categories, not a verified person identity or proof of bank ownership. When either kind is `unknown` or they differ, the matcher suppresses automatic candidates and reports an incomplete result even if the date window has full statement coverage. A cross-recipient confirmation must cite a `recipient_instruction` artifact observed no later than the review event and bound to the selected bank account or no account; a manual marker or an unrelated artifact cannot authorize it. Such a confirmation stays explicitly labeled as source reviewed and unauthenticated. Rejection needs no recipient instruction. The later client review workflow must make this basis legible before it permits confirmation.

Matching results are scoped to the selected bank account. If the whole refund was allocated by review to a different bank account, the selected-account result is `MATCHED_ON_OTHER_ACCOUNT_BY_REVIEW` with no confirmed allocations for this account. It must not claim `MATCHED_BY_REVIEW` for the selected account.

The synthetic golden case has approved school credits `650000` cents and charges `500000` cents, giving `150000` cents. A reviewed grant correction of `-30000` cents and a new charge of `30000` cents yield `90000` cents. The exact delta `-60000` cents has two `-30000` contributions. Refund issuance and bank observation are separate facts.
